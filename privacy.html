Privacy

Last updated October 14, 2026.

Till doesn't collect your data. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We never see your tips, shifts, pay or tax details.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter is stored on your iPhone, inside Till's own storage. It is included in your iPhone's iCloud or computer backup if you have backups turned on, which Apple encrypts and manages. Till keeps up to 30 daily snapshots on the device so an app update can't wipe your history.

What leaves your phone

Only what you choose to share: a tax report, spreadsheet or backup file you export and send yourself. Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store; Till only learns whether Till Pro is active.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to us.

Deleting your data

Settings → Erase all shifts and jobs removes everything from the device. Deleting the app removes it too.
