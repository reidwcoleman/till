Which tips count as tax-free?

Voluntary cash and card tips in an occupation on the Treasury's tipped-occupations list, up to $25,000 a year (less above $150,000 of income, or $300,000 filing jointly). Tips you pay out to coworkers and automatic service charges don't count. Till subtracts tip-outs and tracks service charges separately.

How is overtime worked out?

Only the premium counts: the extra half of time-and-a-half for hours over 40 in a week (or over 8 in a day, if you choose that rule). Add your hourly rate to a job and Till estimates it every week. When your W-2 arrives, enter its box 12 code TT amount and Till uses that instead.

Moving to a new iPhone

Till's data comes along in your iPhone backup. You can also export a backup file (Settings → Export a backup) and restore it on the new phone.

Restoring Till Pro

Settings → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Cancelling

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → Till.

Till keeps records and estimates; it isn't tax advice.
